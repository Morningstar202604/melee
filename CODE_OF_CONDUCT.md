# Code of Conduct

**English** · [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## The short version

Be decent to people. Argue about code, evidence and design — not about the person who wrote it.

## What we expect

- Assume competence and good intent until there is evidence otherwise.
- Critique the work: point at the line, the behaviour, the test, the trade-off.
- Respect different levels of experience and different native languages. Clear writing is welcome; perfect English is not required.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic. Off-topic campaigning, recruiting or advertising is not welcome.

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults, slurs, or “jokes” aimed at a person or a group.
- Harassment in public or private, including unwanted attention after a clear request to stop.
- Sexualised content or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption: derailing threads, reopening settled decisions to exhaust people, or flooding reviews.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer through the repository (a private note via GitHub is fine). Reports are read in confidence. Responses range from a private warning, to removing a comment, to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces. The maintainer will explain the decision to the person involved unless doing so exposes the reporter.

If the maintainer is the problem, say so in the report; another maintainer or a neutral contributor will handle it.
